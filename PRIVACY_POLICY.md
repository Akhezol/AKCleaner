# AKCleaner Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 01/10/2025**

AKCleaner respects user privacy. This application does **not collect, process, or share any personal data** with third parties.

---

## Purpose of the Application
AKCleaner only cleans the following on your device:  
- **Temporary files (Temp folder)**  
- **Recent files list (Recent files)**  

to help you free up storage space.  

---

## User Responsibility
You should **not store important files** in these folders (Temp and Recent).  
AKCleaner deletes the files in these areas, and **deleted files cannot be recovered**.  

---

## Data Collection
- The application **does not collect any personal information**.  
- The application **does not send or share any data over the internet**.
